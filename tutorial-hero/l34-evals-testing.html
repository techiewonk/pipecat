Lesson 34 · Part VI — Production & quality

Evals & testing

⏱ 45 minLevel: intermediate → advanced

Voice agents are hard to test by hand. Pipecat ships an eval framework that scripts conversations, runs your bot headless, and judges the results — plus plain unit testing for processors.

Script, run, judge

The EvalTransport is a headless transport (you saw EvalTransportParams in every example's transport_params) — it drives the pipeline in CI with no microphone or browser.

A scenario + judge

# evals/ provides the pieces: Scenario, the harness/runner, and a Judge.
from pipecat.evals.scenario import Scenario
from pipecat.evals.judge import Judge

scenario = Scenario(
    name="weather_tool",
    turns=["What's the weather in Paris?"],
    expect="the bot calls the weather tool and states a temperature",
)
# the harness runs your bot() against EvalTransport, captures the transcript,
# and the Judge (an LLM) decides pass/fail against `expect`.

Unit-testing processors

A FrameProcessor is ordinary Python — feed it frames and assert what it pushes. No transport needed.

async def test_censor():
    proc = Censor(banned="secret")
    out = await run_processor(proc, [TranscriptionFrame(text="the secret code")])
    assert "secret" not in out[0].text
