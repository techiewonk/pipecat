Runner & telephony

⏱ 45 minLevel: intermediate

The dev runner is how every example boots and picks a transport. Understand it, and putting your bot on a phone number is a config change.

How a bot boots

One server serves WebRTC, Daily, telephony, and WebSocket simultaneously; the client picks via the request. Your bot(runner_args) is the single entry point — the same one Pipecat Cloud calls.

Telephony = WebSocket + a serializer

Phone providers stream μ-law audio over a WebSocket in their own JSON format. A FrameSerializer converts between that and Pipecat frames; the runner auto-wires the right one.

from pipecat.serializers.twilio import TwilioFrameSerializer
transport = FastAPIWebsocketTransport(websocket=ws, params=FastAPIWebsocketParams(
    audio_in_enabled=True, audio_out_enabled=True, add_wav_header=False,
    serializer=TwilioFrameSerializer(stream_sid=..., call_sid=..., account_sid=..., auth_token=...),
))
ngrok http 7860                                            # expose your server
