Lesson 21 · Part IV — Structure, control & capture

Context summarization & strategies

⏱ 35 minLevel: intermediate

Long conversations blow past token limits and cost money. Summarization compresses old turns into a compact summary so the bot keeps the gist without carrying every word.

Compress the old, keep the recent

Context strategies

How much history carries forward is a ContextStrategy, used both in summarization and in Flows node transitions:

StrategyEffect
APPENDKeep everything (default) — grows unbounded.
RESETStart fresh (e.g. entering a new flow node).
RESET_WITH_SUMMARYStart fresh but seed with a summary of what came before.

Wiring summarization

from pipecat.processors.aggregators.llm_context_summarizer import LLMContextSummarizer
# summarize when the context exceeds a token budget:
summarizer = LLMContextSummarizer(llm=llm, token_threshold=4000)
pipeline = Pipeline([transport.input(), stt, user_agg, summarizer, llm, tts,
                     transport.output(), assistant_agg])
# gated context (hold frames until a condition) lives alongside in gated_llm_context.py
